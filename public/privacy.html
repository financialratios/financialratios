Who we are

What we collect

  • No account, no sign-up. You can use every feature without giving us personal data.
  • Searches (ticker symbols) are sent to our server to fetch public company data from our data providers. We do not link them to you.
  • Preferences and your portfolio (in the portfolio tracker) are stored only in your own browser (local storage). They are never sent to us; only the ticker symbols are used to fetch current prices.
  • Reviews: if you write a review, the name (or nickname), rating and text you enter are stored and shown publicly on the Reviews page. We do not ask for your email. Ask us at the contact address to remove a review.
  • Technical logs kept by our hosting provider (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability, for a short period.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a consent banner before personalised ads are shown.

Visitor statistics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see how the site is used (for example which pages are read, which companies are analyzed and how often files are downloaded). Google Analytics uses cookies and receives your IP address, browser and device information; we do not send it your name or email. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through the same consent banner as for ads. You can also install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Affiliate links

Some links may be affiliate links: if you sign up through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. They will be clearly marked.
