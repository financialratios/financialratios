Your money plan in 5 steps

Most people manage money in the wrong order: they invest before they have a safety net, or save "whatever is left" — which is usually nothing. This section is a plan you can follow from top to bottom: protect yourself first, take control of income and spending, clear expensive debt, and only then make your money grow.

👴 In one sentence Cushion first, plan second, debt third, goals fourth — and then let investing do the slow, boring work for decades.

Why this order?

The logic of the plan each step protects the next
1. Emergency fundfirst
Without a cushion, any surprise — a car repair, losing a job — goes on a credit card or forces you to sell investments at a bad moment. Everything else is built on this.
2. Income & expensesknow
You can't plan what you haven't measured. This step tells you how much you can save each month — the fuel for steps 4 and 5.
3. Budget methoddecide
A simple system that tells every dollar where to go, so saving happens automatically instead of by willpower.
4. Debt & goalsclear
Paying off a 24% credit card is a guaranteed 24% return. No investment reliably beats that, so expensive debt goes before investing.
5. Investinggrow
Only money you won't need for 5+ years should go into shares. Now time and compound interest work for you.
