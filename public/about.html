About us

Finance for all, all for finance

Financial Rat was born from a simple frustration: to learn about investing you have to jump between one website for definitions, another for calculators, another for company data and another for charts — each written for experts.

We put the three things you need in one place, in plain language:

  1. Learn — financial statements, ratios and valuation explained from scratch, with real company examples.
  2. Calculators — compound interest, mortgages, returns and more, each with its formula and sources.
  3. Analyze a company — type a ticker and get its business, revenue sources, 10 years of numbers, ratios, valuation and price history.

Our promise: numbers, not opinions

We never tell you to buy or sell. Every analysis is calculated from the company's own reported history, and every assumption is shown and adjustable. You stay in charge of your decisions.

Why a rat?

Financial Ratios — and rats are famously clever, curious and resourceful. That's what we hope you'll feel when using the site.
